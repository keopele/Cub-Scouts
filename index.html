About Our Pack

Pack 2008 is a family-volunteer-led Cub Scout pack based in Cedar Park, TX. We welcome boys and girls in grades K–5th to participate in fun, skill-building activities that promote character, citizenship, and leadership.

We meet weekly and hold exciting events like camping trips, the Raingutter Regatta, the Pinewood Derby, and hands-on service projects.

Our Pack is made up of kids and parent volunteers from Westside, Knowles, and Bagdad Elementary schools, as well as homeschool and charter school families across the Cedar Park and Leander areas.
